Mazetastic Colors combines maze navigation with color-based puzzles. You spin with Q and E, and you spin super fast to lose your color. Line up with the protrusions to open doors and find the exit.

## Process

Playtests found the colors too similar and the spins hard to read. I raised the palette contrast and added clear spin telegraphs.

## Challenges

Designing levels that balanced maze complexity with color puzzle difficulty without overwhelming players, as well as using colors that players could differentiate.

## Outcome and lessons

This project taught me about puzzle design principles and creating intuitive yet challenging gameplay.
