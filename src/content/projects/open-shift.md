## Design

Open Shift is a co-op cozy-horror comedy storekeeping game in development at Pentad Games. I am the lead designer, lead programmer, and product owner. It is entering beta, with demos built and teasers and community building coming up.

## Process

In playtests, the constant scares stopped working: players went numb to them. I paced them out so each scare has room to land. I run playtests regularly, compile what players did, and turn it into design revisions. The team gets a playable build every week.

## Outcome and lessons

Open Shift is still in development, so there is no final outcome yet. So far it taught me that scares only work when they are spaced out.
