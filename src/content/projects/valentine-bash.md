Valentine Bash is a vertical shooter where you defend love by eliminating waves of bugs that threaten to destroy Valentine's Day. It uses multi-camera rendering, and progression is tied to color saturation.

## Challenges

Implementing smooth multi-camera rendering while maintaining consistent performance across different devices.

## Outcome and lessons

This project improved my understanding of camera systems and performance optimization in Unity.
