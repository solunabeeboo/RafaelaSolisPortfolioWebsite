My primary web development projects are this portfolio site, and my personal tool and website Internship-hunter. Both have taught me a massive amount about good practice, literal design & implementation, and the importance of being open to new libraries and methods. Through developing these two sites, I have become skilled in implementing databases, using JavaScript features, following good SEO practices, developing with Astro & Next.js, and more.

Key Features:
- Well presented databases
- Dynamic Creation and Update Tools
- Clean SEO & Performance Practices
- User Centered Design
- Strong Future-proofing

## Challenges

A specific and ongoing challenge is presenting a large assortment of portfolio projects in a concise and discernible manner. This has brought in design, implementation, and backed considerations that require significant and constant thought into how it is made.

## Outcome and lessons

Building these websites has connected the bridge between design & implementation, and has given me a large amount of familiarity with current & useful tools in web development like Astro, JavaScript, Lighthouse, Screaming Frog, and more.
