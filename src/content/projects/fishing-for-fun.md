In Fishing For Fun you use a harpoon both to fight and to get around. You swing through the level, collect fish, and upgrade your stats.

## Challenges

Creating responsive harpoon physics that felt good for both movement and combat, as well as modifying Unreal template Blueprints to inject the new systems, all within the multiplayer Lyra framework.

## Outcome and lessons

This project improved my understanding of physics-based gameplay and arcade game design principles, and taught me a lot about the quirks of Unreal Engine.
