Rat Attack is a game about subverting expectation, and being open to change. The player takes the body of a seasoned exterminator in upstate New York, going into a job he will never forget. Through moments of fast-paced and challenging combat of trying to capture feral rats interlaced with longer calmer sections of storied exploration, the game intends to create a curious and captivating experience. This makes the game suitable for ages 10+ with a focus on the young adult / teen market, meant to be played on PC. It supports keyboard and controller.

## Process

This academic project involved a massive amount of documentation, from creating multiple concepts and whittling them down, to visual system diagrams, and an entire GDD. After the first playtests, the game was too dark and not tense enough, so I added audio cues, made the rats faster, and gave them spline-driven random-intent movement.

## Challenges

A large challenge with Rat Attack was getting down an aesthetic that would create a unique and immersive atmosphere, without being too scopey or nauseating for the player.

## Outcome and lessons

This game helped connect all of the concepts between design and implementation, while allowing me to create a product I'm incredibly proud of.
